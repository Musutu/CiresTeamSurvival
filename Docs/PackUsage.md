# Pack usage (pack-usage passes #2 and #3, 2026-09-27)

Eric bought Fab packs and wants them used. His audit rule: after integrating, count how much of each pack is referenced
(assets per pack / sub-kit) and report before -> after. The table is generated by `Tools/AuditPackUsage.py --before main`
(referenced = the object path appears in committed data under `Content/Data`, `Art/Audio`, `Art/Fab` or in `Source/`;
counted against the assets installed in the main checkout; the packs themselves stay local and are never committed).

Selection rule (Eric, this pass): **quality first**. The hero-grade systems (Shadow Magic, Earth Spells, Forest, State VFX,
Realistic Blood and the best of the Big Pack) lead; when two spells want the same good effect it is reused with a recolour
(`tint` / `tintStrength` on the FabVFX.json entry: every exposed colour parameter of the system keeps its brightness and
moves to the tint's hue, white flashes stay white) instead of falling back to a weaker system. Every pick is recorded per
ability and role in `Content/Data/FabVFX.json` (`abilities.<id>.<role>` = system, scale, tint), so it is reviewable, and
every system's rating lives in `Art/Fab/FabVFXInventory.json` (`quality` A/B/C, `look`, measured `reach`, colour parameters).

## Pass #3 (feat/pack-usage-3, playtest 6)

Before = main after pass #2; after = this branch. The Big Pack / State / Blood percentages dip on purpose: the 105 systems
rated C on the catalogue sheets (weak, nearly invisible, or broken materials such as the Forest nature explosions) were
swapped for A/B systems of the same theme (`FabMonsterVFXTable.DEMOTED`), and the unused A/B systems were wired first
(`POOL_ADDITIONS`, `PACK_USAGE_3_OVERRIDES`). What still sits on the shelf is mostly `stage-blank` (projectiles / lines that
need motion to be judged: rate them from a live gallery before using them) or C. Ratings: `Art/Fab/FabVFXInventory.json`
(A 105, B 176, C 105, unrated 152).

- **On-hit swirl (playtest 6):** `NS_Air_Magic_Hit3` (green crescent swirl) was `hit.armor`, i.e. every weapon hit on any
  plate / mail target, and `NS_Shadow_Magic_Hit2` (teal ribbon swirl) led the grave-green Hollow hit pool. Armour hits are
  now warm steel sparks, a different system per weapon family (+ crits); the swirls remain on whisps, wind / shadow skills.
- **Sizes:** skill effects -10% (`spellEffectScale` 1.17, aura .99, hand 1.08), hit visuals -20% (`hitEffectScale` .89
  on impacts and criticals, on top) in `Content/Data/VFXTuning.json`.
- **Arena portals:** ring, base pool, open and swallow bursts use the real recolour to the arena's hue.

## What was wired where

### Spell VFX (every purchased Niagara / Cascade pack)

- **Monster abilities (334: every race, legacy archetype and Bestiary creature)** now own a signature per presentation
  role (`Tools/FabMonsterVFXTable.py` -> `FabVFX.json` `abilities`). Each race draws on a kit chosen by theme and quality:
  Voidborn = Shadow Magic (+ dark magic), Stoneborn = the full 49 Earth Spells (runic abilities in cyan), Feral Kin = Earth
  Spells quakes / stomps + Forest spirits + red claws, Blightwood = poison + forest vines (roots burst as green-tinted earth
  spikes), Drowned Deep = water (+ navy-tinted ink, teal madness), Ironhide = blood magic + earth + real blood, Drakkari =
  fire (+ ember-tinted molten stone), Hollow = shadow magic in grave-green, Fallen Order = the holy set recoloured to a
  profane violet, Aetheri = lightning / air in aether cyan. Inside one unit no two abilities share a look: a repeat takes a
  recolour variant from the race palette (`CireFabVFX::RunTests` enforces both rules).
- **Effect-type colour language:** what hits the victim is tinted by the crowd control it applies whatever the race
  (root = living green, silence = magenta, stun = gold, slow = ice blue, charm = teal, blind = ink navy: `CC_TINT`).
- **Monster wind-ups** (the "gather" telegraph on circle casts) now flare on the caster with the ability's cast system; the
  telegraph itself stays the procedural, shape-true footprint.
- **Hits, crits and kills (Realistic Blood + debris):** a physical hit with no ability of its own (basic attacks, monster
  melee, weapon strikes) picks `hit.<layer>.<weapon>` by the target's body (flesh / armour / stone / bark / none) and the
  attacker's weapon: slashes for blades, stabs for spears / daggers / arrows, splashes for maces, bullet hits for guns;
  Low intensity on hits, Med / High only on criticals (`.crit`), sparks on plate, debris on stone, leaves on bark, a spirit
  puff on whisps. A death spawns one kill burst by body class (`kill.humanoid / creature / golem / ethereal / boss`,
  `UCireMonsterArt::MulticastDeath`), never per hit. Champion criticals may carry their own `<id>.crit` signature.
- **Level-up** flourish on the champion (`level_up`, `ACireHUD` level detection) beside the procedural burst.
- **Scale:** every decorative system follows `VFXTuning.json` `spellEffectScale` (1.3) exactly as before; fitted ground
  overlays keep their true-radius fit and the ground-brightness slider; cones, lines and polygons keep the procedural
  telegraph alone (a vendor blob can never misstate the hit shape).

### Recolour variants

`CireFabVFX::Recolor` (new): per exposed `LinearColor` user parameter (Lord Enot exposes one per part: `Color_Trail`,
`Color_Sparks`, `Color_Smoke`...), the HSV value (and HDR intensity) and alpha are kept and hue / saturation move to the
tint by `tintStrength`; parameters with saturation < .12 (white flashes, grey smoke) are left alone. The old
`ApplyTint` (generic "Color" parameter names, which none of these packs expose) is kept only for the arena portals, whose
look Eric asked not to change.

### Sound (Fantasy UI SFX)

Purchased pack sounds win everywhere (Eric's ruling); the shipped CC0 / synthesized sounds are the no-pack fallback only.
New in this pass (`Tools/MapFabAudio.py` -> `Art/Audio/FabAudioMap.json` -> `AudioCues.json` `pack` lists):

- new cues: `ui_toast` (every toast chimes unless another shop sound just carried it), `ui_shop_close` (the shop / vendor
  window closing had no sound);
- the four banners that still played only the shipped horn / drums now take the pack (`banner_wave` boom hits,
  `banner_boss` long boom pads, `banner_arena` the Oriental set's ethnic drums, `banner_challenge` impact hits), on top of
  the existing stings;
- more takes for menus, loot, vendors, Skill Shop, ready check, level-up (Generic / Light / Cute / Oriental variants next
  to the Dark base kit).
