# Fab coverage audit (2026-09-25)

Eric asked: *"Did we add all the new FAB assets? All animations added and used? New skill effects on all the spells?
Sounds on all effects, hits, loot, attacks, movement?"* This page is the hard answer: every champion, monster, ability,
buff, sound cue and icon, where its art or sound comes from, what was filled on `feat/fab-coverage`, and what is left.

Art direction for every pick: vibrant, fun, crisp. Only assets Eric already owns were used (no downloads, no new
packs); Tripo is the only source for new models and is listed where a gap needs it.

The animation and spell-VFX tables are generated by `python Tools/AuditFabCoverage.py`, which mirrors the runtime
lookups (`CireFabAnimation::Pick`, `CireFabVFX::FindFor` / `FindBuff`, `CireMonsterArt`) and checks every referenced
asset on disk. The "before" column is the same audit run on `main` (`--before main`).

## A. Animation

### What was filled

- **Champion skills now have their own clips.** Every GDH / Gun & Sword weapon set gained 2-3 standalone heavy
  strikes (`<set>_skill1..3`; Gun & Sword "Skill" attacks for the Gunblade), so ability casts stop replaying the
  basic-attack combo. The combo stays listed after them as the fallback.
- **Shouts, roars, guards and stances** (`war_cry`, `iron_guard`, `bear_roar`, `chieftain_banner`...: the
  `shoutSkills` list) play the Gun & Sword **Buff** clip (weapon raised, chest out) on every humanoid body instead of the
  single Tripo `war_cry` clip.
- **Heals and spells cast by weapon champions** (Paladin, Knight, Keeper...) play the GDH SpellCombat **Heal** clip
  instead of the Tripo `cast_a_spell`.
- **Dodge rolls** for the spear (Lancer, Huntress: Spear V2 roll), gun (Gunblade: Gun & Sword Roll Combat) and unarmed
  (ether golems: Unarmed V2 roll) sets, which had none (procedural tumble before).
- **Troll Berserker (Ranged)** had no Fab row at all: its axe throws now use the GDH Dagger **Throw** clips (extra set on
  the troll body), with the dual-axe set for hits, deaths, rolls and skills.
- **Monsters:** Tripo monster bodies whose rallies, enrages, guards, pulls, summons or self-circles had no shout or slam
  clip (47 bodies) get the Gun & Sword Buff (`war_cry`) and Air-to-Floor strike (`ground_slam`) retargeted onto them
  (`Content/Data/MonsterFabClips.json`, read by `CireMonsterArt`; added only where the body has no clip of that name).
  Fab creature units: the Barghest (`grave_hound`) snarls for Pack Howl, the boar (`bristleback`) rears for Bristle Burst.

Tools: `Tools/BuildFabAnimMap.py` (sets, skills, shouts, casts, rolls, throw set, monster bodies) ->
`Tools/RetargetFabAnimations.py -CireFabAnimKeep` (champions) and `-CireFabAnimMonsters` (monsters).

## B. Spell VFX

### What was filled

- **One signature system per ability and role.** `Content/Data/FabVFX.json` gained `abilities.<id>.<role>` (from
  `Tools/FabAbilityVFXTable.py` through `Tools/MapFabVFX.py`); `CireFabVFX::FindFor` tries it before the school set, and
  an entry whose pack is missing falls back to the school set, then to the procedural art. Picks follow the ability's
  school and fantasy (e.g. Frost Bind: ice spear + spear shatter; Cataclysm: fire circle, Kakky explosion and fire
  storm; Keeper's Dawn Beam: Kakky laser + holy sword lane).
- **Physical skills are no longer bare.** The steel school had only an impact; it now has a crisp wind-slash cast and
  wind arrow, and every physical champion skill has its own slash / stab / shockwave.
- **FX Variety Pack mapped** (Cascade, 9 of its 21 systems after gallery review): explosion (Cataclysm), fire storm,
  heal aura (Renewal, Sanctuary), laser (Dawn Beam), shooting stars (Starfall), muzzle shockwave (Silver Shot), wind
  storm (Glaive Storm), thunder-ball hit (Phase Lance), water-ball hit (Wellspring). `CireFabVFX` resolves
  `UFXSystemAsset` and spawns Niagara or Cascade (`UFXSystemComponent`), at 0.6 scale (UE4 demo scale).
- **Every buff / debuff has its own overlay** (`buffs.<id>`), except the always-on passives and the eleven level-15
  party auras, which keep only their procedural signature on purpose: a looping Fab effect on every ally for the whole
  match would drown the combat read.
- **Telegraphs unchanged:** warnings (enemy lanes, gathers, void zones, area wind-ups) still never get an overlay, so
  they stay the dim procedural fill and keep the true hit shape; the intensity slider still scales other units' auras.
