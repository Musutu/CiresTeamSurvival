# Launch Intro Cinematic — "The Sundering of Emberhold"

A two-part launch intro for **Cire's Team Survival**. The tone is realistic, mature, gritty dark fantasy. There's no dialogue, only an orchestral score and battle sound effects.

> **Status (2026-09-25):** not generated yet. The original target, OpenAI Sora 2, has been shut down. `sora.chatgpt.com` redirects to `/sunset` ("Sora is no longer available"), and ChatGPT Pro has no video tool. The prompts below don't depend on any one tool. Eric will choose among Veo 3, Runway, Kling and Luma (see §6).

## 1. Storyline

**Part 1 — "The Rift Opens."** Dusk falls over Emberhold, a walled medieval town with Ember Keep, a gothic castle, on the hill. Above the fields outside the gate the sky tears open into a violet-black rift, and the Hollow march out of it. They're led by Gravemaw the wolf-headed warlord, with Drowned Deep cultists and treants behind them. Bells toll and townsfolk flee toward the keep. One by one, five champions arrive at the gate: the Iron Warden, the Ash Ranger, the Veil Scholar, the Dusk Lancer and the Gravewood Bear. They end standing shoulder to shoulder as the horde charges.

**Part 2 — "Hold the Gate."** The horde hits the line. The Warden's shield takes the first blow, arrows cut down undead, and the Scholar raises a golden dome. The Bear smashes a treant and the Lancer impales a cultist. Gravemaw duels the Warden, and the Lancer and the Warden bring him down together. The rift pulses and more shapes begin to emerge. The final shot shows the five champions raising their weapons before the glowing keep while embers drift through the air.

## 2. Style & cast block (paste VERBATIM at the start of every prompt, both parts)

```
STYLE: Photoreal dark-fantasy cinematic, AAA game-trailer quality, Unreal Engine 5 look. Dusk lighting: deep orange sunset low on the horizon fighting a cold violet glow from a rift in the sky. Anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, subtle lens flares, 24 fps with fine film grain, desaturated teal-and-ember color grade. 16:9 widescreen. No on-screen text, titles, logos, subtitles or watermarks.

SETTING: Emberhold, a walled medieval town of timber-framed houses and a cobbled market square, with a massive arched stone town gate with an iron portcullis, and Ember Keep, a dark gothic castle with lit windows, on the hill behind the town. Outside the gate: open fields under a violet-black swirling rift torn in the sky, crackling with purple lightning.

THE FIVE CHAMPIONS (always exactly these five, same look in every shot):
1. IRON WARDEN: knight in full polished silver-steel plate armour, closed great helm with a narrow eye slit, deep crimson tabard with a gold emblem, steel longsword, large kite shield.
2. ASH RANGER: hooded archer in dark brown-and-charcoal leather armour, grey hood and short cloak, tall wooden longbow, quiver of arrows on the back.
3. VEIL SCHOLAR: robed healer in flowing white robes with gold trim and a gold-embroidered hood, carrying a tall wooden staff topped with a crystal that glows warm golden light.
4. DUSK LANCER: lean warrior in dark blackened-steel armour with a dark blue cloak, open-faced helm, wielding a long steel spear.
5. GRAVEWOOD BEAR: a massive brown grizzly bear wearing battle-worn iron armour plates strapped over its shoulders, back and head.

THE ENEMY — "THE HOLLOW":
- GRAVEMAW, the warlord: towering wolf-headed humanoid in spiked black plate armour, grey fur cloak and a tattered red cape, wielding a massive double-bladed battle axe, glowing ember-orange eyes.
- Hollow soldiers: gaunt undead warriors in rusted, broken plate armour with glowing ember-orange eyes.
- Drowned Deep cultists: squid-headed humanoids with tentacled faces in dark, dripping sea-green robes.

AUDIO: Epic orchestral score with deep taiko-style war drums, low brass and a dark choir, building in intensity. Battle sound effects only: church bells, wind, thunder from the rift, steel clashes, shield impacts, arrow whooshes, monster roars. NO dialogue, NO speech, NO narration, NO voices other than wordless choir.

TONE: Mature, serious, intense dark fantasy combat. No blood, no gore, no dismemberment, no sexual content. Enemies fall or shatter into ash and embers when defeated.
```
