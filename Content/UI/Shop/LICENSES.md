# Shop sound licenses

`S_ShopBuy, S_ShopSell, S_ShopError, S_ShopUndo, S_ShopOpen, S_ShopTab, S_LootPickup,
S_TeleportChannel, S_TeleportArrive` are original sounds synthesized from sine partials and filtered
noise by `Tools/BuildShopContent.py` (sources in `src/*.wav`). No recorded or third-party audio is
used; no license obligations apply.

# Skill Shop art

`Scrolls/T_Scroll_Golden, T_Scroll_Plain, T_Scroll_Prismatic` and `Scrolls/T_Crest_Active,
T_Crest_Passive, T_Crest_Ultimate` are cut from Eric's own reference image
`Saved/Reference/skills-target.png` (generated for Eric via ChatGPT; used with his permission) by
`Tools/CutSkillScrolls.py` (sources in `Scrolls/src/*.png`), and imported with mipmaps by
`Tools/BuildSkillShopArt.py`. `Scrolls/T_ShopGlow` is a radial glow generated by that script
(original, no third-party content). No higher-resolution regeneration was needed: the cut scrolls
(~520 px) are drawn at 150-250 px at 1080p.
